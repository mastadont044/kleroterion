Why it cannot be rigged

In Athens, around 370 BC, a slab of stone decided who sat on a jury. Citizens pressed their bronze tokens into its slots, black and white dice ran down the tube beside it, and each die took or dismissed an entire row. It existed so that a seat could not be bought. This is the same machine, with a block hash in place of the dice.

1Before every draw the block it is bound to is published. That block does not exist yet, so its hash is unknown to everyone, this project included.

2When the block arrives, every holder balance is read at that exact block and the full list is published with the result.

3The winner is keccak(blockhash, token, draw#) mod total_weight, walking holders sorted by address. Your weight is your balance.

4Run verify draws/N.json against any past draw and it returns the same winner, or the record is a lie.
