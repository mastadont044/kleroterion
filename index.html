Where the name comes from

In Athens around 370 BC a slab of stone picked juries. People put their bronze tokens into the slots, black and white dice went down the tube on the side, and each die took or dropped a whole row. It existed because a seat on a jury was worth buying.

Same thing here. Your balance is the token in the slot, a block hash replaced the dice, and unlike in Athens you can check the result afterwards yourself.

1Before a draw runs, the block it will use is published. That block has not happened yet, so nobody knows its hash, us included.

2When the block lands, every holder balance is read at that exact block and published together with the result.

3The winner is keccak(blockhash, token, draw#) mod total weight, going through holders sorted by address.

4Run verify draws/N.json on any past draw. If it returns a different winner, we lied.
